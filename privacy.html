Privacy Policy

Tumbledown: Domino Puzzle · Effective October 26, 2026

Tumbledown is made by Solut ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game does with information. In short: there are no accounts, we do not collect your name or email through the game, and we do not sell your data.

Information stored on your device

Your progress (cleared levels, stars, daily challenge history and streaks) and your settings (sound, haptics, look and colours) are stored on your device. Progress is also synced between your own devices using Apple iCloud key-value storage. We cannot see this data; it is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Deleting the app removes the local copy, and you can manage iCloud data in your device's iCloud settings.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game reports your achievements and leaderboard scores (levels cleared and your best daily streak) to Apple Game Center. Game Center is operated by Apple and is covered by Apple's privacy policy. You can change what is shared in iOS Settings, then Game Center.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of Tumbledown shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (only if you allow tracking), IP address, device and operating system information, and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses data from ads and Google's Privacy Policy.

The first time you open the game, iOS asks whether the app may track you across other companies' apps and websites. You can choose "Ask App Not to Track" and ads will still appear, but they will be less personalised. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings, then Privacy & Security, then Tracking. Buying Remove Ads turns ads off.

Purchases

The optional Remove Ads subscription is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. The game only checks, on your device, whether your Apple ID owns the subscription.

Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification created on your device. It does not use a server and no information is sent anywhere. You can turn it off in iOS Settings, then Notifications, then Tumbledown.

Children

Tumbledown is a general-audience puzzle game and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above. Continued use of the game after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Tumbledown: Domino Puzzle

Support and frequently asked questions

Need help? Email jomarshal.music@gmail.com and tell us your iPhone model, your iOS version and what happened. We usually reply within a couple of days.

How do I play?

The gold domino falls first. Tap an empty square to place a domino, tap a domino to turn it, and hold one to pick it up. Press GO to start the chain. Knock down every red domino (debt) to clear the level and keep the blue dominoes standing. Stone blocks stop the chain. You can reopen the tutorial any time from Settings, then How to play.

What is the Daily Challenge?

A new puzzle every day, the same for everyone. Clear it to build your streak and fill in the monthly calendar. Clear 10, 20 or every day of a month for a bronze, silver or gold trophy.

How do I remove ads?

Open the Settings tab, then Subscription, then Remove Ads. It is an optional auto-renewing subscription ($1.99 per year in the US; the price shows in your local currency). It removes banners and ad breaks, and hints and extra dominoes become free.

I bought Remove Ads but still see ads

Open Settings, then Subscription, then Restore purchase, while signed in to the Apple ID you used to buy it.

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Tumbledown and tap Cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. Apple handles all billing and refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is my progress saved?

Yes. Your progress and settings are stored on your device. If you sign in to Game Center on a new device, the game can restore the levels you had cleared (at one star each). Game Center also keeps your achievements and leaderboard scores.

How do I stop the daily reminder?

The reminder is an ordinary notification. Turn it off in iOS Settings, then Notifications, then Tumbledown.

Sound or vibration stopped working

Check that Sound and Haptics are on in the Settings tab and that your phone is not on silent. If sound stops after a call, close and reopen the app. If it keeps happening, email us.
